# FAQ and limitations
**What is a ping?** A Taiwanese unit of area, 3.3058 m². Prices are in units of 10,000 NT$ per ping.

**Why is the range not 90%?** The quantile models cover 76% of held-out sales; the range is shown as indicative.

**Can I use it for other cities?** No. The data cover one district in 2012–2013.

**Limitations:** 414 sales; no floor area, floor level, building type or renovation data; prices from over a decade ago.
